# MRR Estimations

Three scenarios — conservative, base, and aggressive — projecting MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue) and total monthly revenue over 12 months. The math is shown so you can sub in your own assumptions.

> **Note on terminology:** Strictly speaking, MRR refers only to the subscription line. But for a gift-heavy product like StorySpark, the meaningful metric is **total monthly revenue** (TMR) — one-time + recurring combined. This doc tracks both.

---

## Core assumptions (across scenarios)

| Variable | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Avg. order value (AOV) | $31 | 70% hardcover, 30% digital, plus add-ons |
| Gross margin (blended) | 62% | weighted avg of digital, hardcover, sub |
| Subscription price | $19/month | |
| Subscription gross margin | 88% | $16.81 monthly profit per sub |
| Subscription churn | 8% / month | typical for content-light D2C subs |
| Subscription attach rate | 4% of buyers | grows with cohort retention efforts |
| One-time → repeat purchase | 0.35 books / customer / yr | gift-givers buy once; parents buy multiple |
| Conversion rate (visitor → buyer) | 1.5% (M1) → 3.0% (M12) | tightens as creative + landing page mature |

## How the math works

For any month:

```
buyers = visitors × conversion_rate
one_time_revenue = buyers × AOV
new_subs = buyers × subscription_attach_rate
active_subs(month_n) = active_subs(month_n-1) × (1 - churn) + new_subs
sub_MRR = active_subs × $19
total_monthly_revenue = one_time_revenue + sub_MRR
```

The compounding of `active_subs` is what makes the subscription line grow much faster than visitor traffic in later months — it's the long-term business.

## Sensitivity analysis

What moves the needle most? Ranked by leverage:

| Lever | Impact on Y1 revenue if doubled | How to move it |
|---|---|---|
| **Conversion rate** | +95% | Better preview UX, social proof, AOV upsells |
| **AOV** | +95% | Push hardcover, add-ons, gift wrapping |
| **Visitor traffic** | +95% | Content + paid ads + creator seeding |
| **Subscription attach** | +18% (Y1), +60% (Y2+) | Onboarding flow, "next book" prompts |
| **Repeat purchase rate** | +28% | Email automation, birthday triggers |
| **Churn (down 50%)** | +12% (Y1), +40% (Y3+) | Better content, family profiles |

**The three things to obsess over:** conversion, AOV, and traffic quality. Everything else is incremental.

---

## When to reforecast

- After your first 100 orders — you'll have real conversion + AOV numbers
- After your first paid ad spend — you'll have real CAC
- After 3 months of subscription data — you'll have real churn

Until then, treat these numbers as a planning tool, not a prediction.
