# Marketing Playbook

A go-to-market plan for StorySpark, organized by stage. The product has obvious viral potential (parents love sharing photos of their kids), so the plan leans heavily into **organic + creator-driven** acquisition before paid.

---

## 1. Positioning

**One-line pitch:** "A picture book where your child is the hero — made in 60 seconds, treasured for years."

**Who it's for (in priority order):**

1. **Gift-givers** — aunts, uncles, grandparents, family friends. Highest willingness to pay, lowest price sensitivity. Birthdays, holidays, baby showers.
2. **Parents of 3–8 year olds** — bedtime ritual, screen-time alternative, milestone moments (first day of school, new sibling).
3. **Teachers & librarians** — bulk orders for class sets, end-of-year gifts. (Long-tail B2B.)
4. **Therapists & counselors** — bibliotherapy for anxiety, grief, fears. Niche but high-value.

**What makes us different:** speed (under a minute), quality (not just mad-libs — real story arcs with theme + outcome), and the keepsake hardcover that turns a digital product into something a kid sleeps with for years.

---

## 2. Channels (in launch order)

### Phase 1 — Months 0–3: Foundation + organic

**TikTok / Instagram Reels (primary)**
- Format: "I made my niece a personalized book in 60 seconds — her reaction broke me 😭"
- The reaction shot is the entire campaign. One viral video = thousands of orders.
- Cadence: 5–7 posts/week. Re-cut the same content 4 different ways.
- Hashtags: `#giftideas`, `#auntlife`, `#bedtimestories`, `#momsoftiktok`, `#booktok`.

**Pinterest (high-intent, evergreen)**
- Boards: "Gifts for the kid who has everything," "Bedtime routines that work," "Personalized baby gifts."
- Pin every blog post, every customer photo. Pinterest traffic compounds for years.

**SEO blog content** — publish weekly:
- "37 personalized gifts for a 5-year-old that they'll actually love"
- "How to handle a child's first day of school anxiety (and a book that helps)"
- "Personalized baby shower gifts under $40"
- These rank for high-commercial-intent keywords. Each post links into the generator.

**Reddit (sparingly, authentically)** — `r/daddit`, `r/mommit`, `r/giftideas`, `r/Parenting`. Do not promote. Answer questions, mention the product when truly relevant. One thread can drive 500+ orders if it lands.

### Phase 2 — Months 3–6: Creator partnerships

**Mom influencers (mid-tier, 50k–500k followers)**
- Send a free hardcover featuring their kid's name. Most will post unprompted; that's the play.
- Budget: $0 for the book + $50 shipping × 100 creators = $5,000 to test 100 micro-collabs.
- Conversion expectation: 10–15 produce content, 2–3 drive meaningful sales (~50–200 orders each).

**Bookstagram / BookTok**
- Different audience (the books-as-art crowd). Pitch the keepsake angle and unboxing.

**Niche newsletters** — sponsor:
- "What to Read If" (Anne Bogel)
- "Cup of Jo" (Joanna Goddard) — lifestyle, gift-heavy
- The Skimm Parents
- Cost: $1,500–$8,000 per send. Track with unique discount codes.

### Phase 3 — Months 6–12: Paid + retention

**Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram)**
- Don't run paid until organic CAC is known. Use Meta to scale what's working organically.
- Creative: same UGC reaction videos. Static ads underperform here.
- Target: Lookalike of past purchasers + interests (children's books, baby shower, gift ideas).
- Expected CAC: $18–$30 for a $34 hardcover (margin allows it; LTV from reorders justifies it).

**Google Shopping / Search**
- Bid on "personalized children's book," "custom kids book gift," "book with my child's name."
- These are buying-intent keywords. CPC: $1.50–$4. Conversion rate: 4–8%.

**Email** — every order becomes a customer. Send:
- 3-day post-purchase: "Did the book arrive? Reply with a photo for 20% off your next."
- 30-day: "Their next adventure?" (personalized recommendation)
- Birthday automation (you have their kid's profile): "Theo turns 5 next month — make him a new book?"

---

## 3. Content engine

The single most important channel is **UGC reaction videos**. Build the entire content engine around generating them:

1. **Make UGC easy.** Inside every shipped hardcover, include a small printed card: "Filming their reaction? Tag us — we send you a free book."
2. **Repost everything.** Every reaction video on the brand account, with permission. This tells future customers what reaction they'll get.
3. **Encourage gift videos.** "The look on her face" is the entire pitch.

---

## 4. Partnerships

- **Pediatricians' offices** — supply free demo books for waiting rooms. Sticker on the back: "Make one for your child."
- **Daycares & preschools** — fundraiser model: parents order books, school keeps 20%. Free customer acquisition.
- **Children's hospitals** — donate books for long-stay patients. Brand-positive PR + emotional content.
- **Subscription boxes** — KiwiCo, Lovevery, Bookroo — bundle a digital code in their boxes.
- **Etsy shops** that sell baby gifts — affiliate link to your product alongside their handmade items.

---

## 5. Launch checklist (first 30 days)

- [ ] Set up analytics (Plausible or PostHog — privacy-friendly is on-brand for parents)
- [ ] Install pixel for Meta + Google for retargeting (even before running ads)
- [ ] Email capture on the homepage (10% off first order)
- [ ] 30 TikToks recorded and scheduled
- [ ] 50 Pinterest pins published
- [ ] 5 SEO blog posts live
- [ ] Send 100 free books to micro-influencers
- [ ] Apply to 3 podcast sponsorships (parenting niche)
- [ ] Set up review collection (Junip or Loox) — social proof drives 2–3x conversion
- [ ] Outreach to 20 mom newsletters

---

## 6. KPIs to watch

| Metric | Target (M3) | Target (M12) |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly visitors | 25,000 | 200,000 |
| Visit → preview conversion | 18% | 25% |
| Preview → purchase | 6% | 10% |
| Avg. order value | $28 | $42 |
| Repeat purchase rate (90d) | 12% | 28% |
| Subscription opt-in (Spark Club) | 4% | 12% |
| CAC (blended) | $14 | $22 |
| LTV (12-mo) | $48 | $115 |

The leading indicator is **preview-to-purchase**. If the generator delights, it converts. If conversion is below 5%, the product itself needs work before more traffic.
